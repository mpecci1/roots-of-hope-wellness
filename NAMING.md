# Naming, frozen

The ladder was renamed three times in one day. This file is the freeze. Changing
anything here means changing the site, the SimplePractice service names and
Elyse's own email wording on the same day.

## The ladder

| Step | Name on the card | What the button says | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Discovery Call | Book a free 15-min call | Free, 15 minutes |
| 2 | The Root Consult | Book the Root Consult | $225, 75 minutes |
| 3, as needed | The Root Review | Book a Root Review | $125, 60 minutes |
| 3, monthly | The Bloom Membership | Become a member | $97 / month |

## Rules

1. **Brand names live on the cards and inside SimplePractice. Buttons use plain
   words.** Nobody falls in love with a screening call, and a made-up noun in the
   one button that finally became clear is a bad trade.
2. **A plain descriptor sits beside every name**, at body size, never in muted
   grey at .85rem.
3. **No two step names start with the same word.** "Root Consult" and "Root
   Review" currently break this. A caller who says "book a Root..." can mean
   either, and the board flagged it as a booking error waiting to happen. Open
   question for Maguire and Elyse: rename step 3 (candidates that clear the
   collision: The Check-in, The Follow-up, The Tending Session).
4. **Banned:** "Deep dive" as a tag, two cards sharing the same "Step 3" label,
   "consultation" as a button label anywhere, "Seed Call".
5. The site never types a price except on the pricing card.

## Kill test, 90 days

If Elyse is writing "discovery call" and "follow-up" in her own emails to
clients, switch the site to plain words permanently rather than argue again.
