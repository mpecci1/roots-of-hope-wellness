Before you book

What does metabolic health mean?
It’s how well your body makes and uses energy: blood sugar, hormones, gut, sleep and stress all working together. Instead of treating each symptom on its own, we look at how that whole system is functioning and address what’s actually driving how you feel.
Are visits virtual?
Yes. Every visit is online, so you can meet from home with nothing but a quiet spot and an internet connection. A nurse practitioner can only see clients in the states where she holds a license, so if you are not sure whether that includes you, ask on your free call and Elyse will tell you straight.
Does this replace my doctor?
No, and it is not meant to. Elyse works alongside your primary care provider, not instead of them, so keep your regular appointments and tell her who else is on your care team. This is not emergency care: if something is urgent, call your doctor or 911.
Do you take insurance?
No. I don’t take insurance directly. All visits are flat self-pay rates: $225 for the Root Consult, $125 for a Root Review.
How soon will I feel better?
Everyone is different, and an honest answer matters more than a fast one. Elyse sets realistic milestones with you at your first visit and adjusts as your body responds. If something is not working, the plan changes rather than waiting it out.
How do I get started?
Book a free 15-minute call. We’ll talk through what you’re hoping for and decide the best first step together. Book your Discovery Call
